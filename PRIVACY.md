# Privacy Policy

**samambaia** — last updated October 6, 2026

samambaia does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Everything stays on your device. The extension makes no network requests, has no servers, and uses no analytics, tracking, or advertising. Backups are files you export yourself, and you decide where they go.

The text expansion feature has to run on any page where you type, so samambaia requests permission to access data on all websites. The expander only watches the text you type in editable fields, locally, to detect your own shortcuts and replace them with your scripts. What you type is not stored, logged, or sent anywhere. You can disable **Text expansion** in the extension. When it is off, the content script is unregistered and no longer runs on any website.
